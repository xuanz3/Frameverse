A new chapter for AnimeJapan: Osaka, 2027

AnimeJapan heads to INTEX Osaka for its 2027 edition. Here is what to know before planning a visit.

FRAMEVERSE EDITORIAL20 NOV 20263 MIN READ
THE STORY

The 2027 edition of AnimeJapan marks a move to INTEX Osaka. For visitors, the change means planning around Osaka rather than the Tokyo venue associated with previous editions.

Before arranging travel, use the organizer’s website for admission announcements, access information and any updates to the event programme.
