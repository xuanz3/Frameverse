FAQ / QUESTIONS

The things
worth asking.

Some of the most common questions about our archive and the culture of collecting.

01 / Are the featured figures available to buy?+

No. The featured FRAMEVERSE character pieces are original visual concepts created for editorial and creative presentation, not commercial inventory.

02 / Where can I verify convention dates?+

Every event listing includes a link to the official organizer. Always verify dates, venues, ticket availability and entry rules before booking travel.

03 / Can I submit an event or suggest a correction?+

Yes. Use the contact page with the event name, official source URL and the update you would like us to review.

04 / What is the difference between PVC and resin?+

PVC is a common, flexible production material for many character figures. Resin and polystone are often used in heavier, highly detailed display statues. Both require sensible handling and storage.

05 / How should I clean a collectible?+

Start with a soft, dry brush or microfiber cloth. Keep moisture and harsh chemicals away from delicate paints, decals and clear parts; follow product-specific guidance where available.

06 / May I reuse FRAMEVERSE images?+

The original visual concepts and editorial presentation are not automatically licensed for third-party reuse. Please contact us to discuss a specific request.
