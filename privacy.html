Privacy and clarity.

How this independent website handles visitor information.

Our approach

FRAMEVERSE does not require you to create an account. This static version does not include advertising trackers, analytics cookies or an on-site user database.

Contacting us

The contact form opens your email application. Your name, email and message are not transmitted to our servers by the form itself. Sending an email is governed by your email provider and the recipient’s email service.

External sites

Convention listings link directly to third-party organizers. Their websites operate under their own terms and privacy practices.

Updates

This policy reflects the initial site release of November 2026 and will be revised if data-processing features are added.
